# KiboFlow Studio — Phone, Walk-In & WhatsApp Sales Playbook

## Golden Rule
**Say less than you want to.** Get permission to send the live demo on WhatsApp, send it within 60 seconds, and **let them scroll in silence**. The phone screen closes the deal.

---

## Step 1: The 20-Second Phone Opener

> "Morning! My name's Mark Munene, founder of KiboFlow Studio here in Nyeri — I'm not selling you anything on this call. I built a mobile website and WhatsApp booking concept for a place like **[Company Name]** and I'd like to send it to your WhatsApp. Is this number okay, or should I use another one?"

**Why it works:**
- Disarms the gatekeeper reflex in the first 6 seconds.
- Never insults their current website ("never say your site is broken").
- Asks for a tiny, frictionless "yes" (permission to WhatsApp a link).

---

## Step 2: The 60-Second WhatsApp Drop

Send immediately while you are fresh in mind:

> Hi **[Name]**, Mark Munene here from KiboFlow Studio — we just spoke.
>
> Open this live mobile concept on your phone and scroll:
> **[Live Demo Link]**
>
> Everything a customer looks for is there in the first 3 seconds: 1-tap call, menu/rates, Google Maps pin, and direct WhatsApp booking/ordering.
>
> Have a look. If you'd like one tailored for **[Company Name]**, I can have it live in 7 days. No pressure either way.

---

## Step 3: Answering "How Much Does It Cost?"

> "Forty-five thousand shillings [or KES 65,000 for full hotel/clinic system]. That covers custom design, mobile optimization, wiring up the 1-tap WhatsApp booking and Google Maps buttons, and launching it live inside 7 days. I take 50% upfront via M-Pesa to lock your build week, and the other half only when it's live and you're happy on your phone."

*Then stop talking and wait for them to respond first.*

---

## Step 4: Handling Price Pushback (Scope-Down, Never Discount)

If a prospect asks for a lower price:

> "At KES 38,000, we can launch the high-converting homepage, Google Maps pin, and 1-tap WhatsApp booking bar this week, and add the multi-page photo gallery and full menu pages later when you're ready."

---

## Step 5: Same-Day 2-Hour Price-Lock Follow-Up

> **[Name]**, thanks again for speaking earlier. The mobile concept stays live here if you want to show your partner/team: **[Live Demo Link]**
>
> I have one 7-day build slot open starting Monday and I'll hold that price for you until Friday at 5 PM.
> — Mark Munene · `0729 213641`

---

## Step 6: The Nyeri Local Unfair Advantage (Walk-In Protocol)

If a top local target (Kenchic Inn on Gakere Rd, Le Pristine on Seremwai Rd, Delight Hotel in Nyeri Town) doesn't pick up:
1. Open the live Hospitality Demo on your phone at 100% brightness.
2. Walk in during off-peak hours (10:30–11:45 AM or 2:30–4:30 PM).
3. Ask politely for the manager/owner, hand them your phone or WhatsApp the link on the spot: *"I'm a software developer right here in Nyeri—I mocked up how customers can book/order from you on WhatsApp in 3 taps."*
