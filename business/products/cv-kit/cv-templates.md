# CV Templates — copy, fill, send

Three templates. Pick ONE. Delete the other two. Replace every `[BRACKET]`.

**Rules that apply to all three:**
- One page if you have under 10 years' experience. Two maximum otherwise.
- Single column. No tables, no text boxes, no images, no headers/footers.
- Save as `Firstname-Lastname-CV.docx`.
- Before every application, re-read Mistake 5 in the guide and swap in the advert's keywords.

## Cover letter templates

### A — Applying to an advertised role

```
[Your name]
[Phone] · [Email] · [Town]

[Date]

[Hiring manager's name if you can find it — otherwise: The Hiring Manager]
[Organisation]
[Town]

RE: Application for [exact job title] — [reference number if given]

Dear [Name / Sir or Madam],

[One sentence: the role and one specific, real reason you want THIS organisation —
name something they actually do.]

[Paragraph two: your single strongest, most relevant achievement, with a number.
Then one sentence on a second relevant skill.]

[Paragraph three: what you would do in your first 90 days, in one sentence.
Then: I have attached my CV and would welcome the chance to discuss the role.]

Yours sincerely,
[Your name]
```

### B — Speculative / walking in / cold WhatsApp

```
Dear [Name],

I am writing because [one specific reason — I saw your vacancy / I use your service /
your branch on [street] is always busy].

I am a [your profession] with [X] years in [field]. Recently I [one achievement with a
number]. I believe I could [the specific value you would add to them].

My CV is attached. If you are not hiring now, I would be grateful for any advice — or
for a pointer to someone who is.

[Your name] · [Phone]
```

### C — After a referral

```
Dear [Name],

[Referrer's name] suggested I contact you about [the role / the opportunity].

[Two sentences: the single most relevant thing you have done, with a number.]

I have attached my CV and am available at [phone] whenever suits you.

[Your name]
```
