## Why this sells

Every funding door in Kenya requires a written business plan:

- **Uwezo Fund** (constituency level)
- **Women Enterprise Fund**
- **Youth Enterprise Development Fund**
- **County government bursaries and SME grants** (Nyeri County included)
- **SACCO business loans**
- **Bank SME loans** (KCB, Equity, Co-op, NCBA)
- **NGO and church-group grants**

Most people never apply because they cannot write the plan. They will pay someone who can. Twice: once for the template, and again if they want it written for them.

## What to ask a done-for-you client (your intake script)

Copy this and send it after they pay:

```
To write your plan I need these. Answer in one WhatsApp message, rough is fine:

1. Full name, ID number, phone, town/estate
2. The business — what exactly do you sell or do?
3. Where will it operate? (market stall / shop / online / mobile)
4. How long have you been running it? (or: when will you start?)
5. How much money do you need, and what EXACTLY will you buy with it?
6. Do you have any of your own money to put in? How much?
7. How many customers do you serve per day/week now?
8. What is your price per unit/service? What does it cost you per unit?
9. Who else does this near you, and what do they charge?
10. Who is helping you run it? (family / employees / alone)
11. Which fund or lender are you applying to? (I'll format it for them)
12. Anything that worries you about the business
```
