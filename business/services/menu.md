## 1. CV Makeover — KES 800 · 24 hours

**Includes**
- Full rewrite of the CV into a single-column ATS-safe layout
- Summary rewritten to be specific (who + proof + number)
- Every bullet rewritten as *verb + what + number*
- Keywords from **up to 3** job adverts the customer supplies
- One matching cover letter
- Delivered as `.docx` **and** PDF
- **One revision round** (within 48 hours of delivery)

**Does not include**
- Writing a CV from nothing (well — it does, but see below)
- LinkedIn profile (that's the combo)
- More than 3 job-advert keyword passes
- More than one revision round

**If they have no CV at all:** same price. Ask for certificates + a plain WhatsApp message describing every job they've had, with dates.

**Your time:** 60–90 minutes.

**Intake message (send the moment they say yes):**
> Perfect! To get started send me:
> 1. Your current CV (or your certificates + a list of every job with dates)
> 2. Up to 3 job adverts you want to apply for — copy the text
> 3. The job title you're targeting
> 4. Your phone number and email as you want them to appear
>
> I'll have it back to you within 24 hours. You pay after you've seen the draft.

**Delivery message:**
> Here's your CV 👇 [file]
> And your cover letter 👇 [file]
> Save as `Firstname-Lastname-CV.docx` when you apply — recruiters do notice.
> One round of changes is included if you spot anything. Anything else, just ask.
> If this was useful, a screenshot I can share would mean a lot 🙏

## Pricing discipline

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Never discount | Offer the cheaper product instead. Discounts train people to wait for the next one. |
| Never work before a deposit on big jobs | KES 500 deposit on the business plan, balance on delivery. |
| Never let "just one more thing" slide | "Happy to — that's a separate piece of work, KES X." |
| Raise prices every 10 customers | Demand is information. Use it. |
| Quote the high tier first | Some will say yes. Nobody has ever upsold themselves. |
