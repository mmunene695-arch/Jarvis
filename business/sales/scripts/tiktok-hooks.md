# 30 TikTok / Reel Hooks

**Format rules that matter more than the script:**
- First 2 seconds decide everything. Say the hook out loud, on camera, looking at the lens.
- Film vertical, in daylight, near a window. Sound matters more than picture — speak clearly.
- Text on screen for every video (most Kenyans watch muted).
- 15–30 seconds. Nobody finishes a 3-minute video from a stranger.
- End every video with the same CTA: *"WhatsApp me, number in my bio."*
- Post **2 per day** — one at 7–8am, one at 7–9pm. Those are the Kenyan peaks.
- Post from your real account, with your real face. Faceless accounts don't sell services.

## The 4-part structure for every video

1. **Hook (0–2s):** the problem, out loud, in their words.
2. **Agitate (2–8s):** why it keeps happening / what it's costing them.
3. **Value (8–22s):** one real, usable tip. Give something away for free.
4. **CTA (22–30s):** *"If you want me to just do it for you — WhatsApp me. Number in my bio. KES 800, 24 hours."*

## What to do when one video pops

If a video passes 5,000 views in 24 hours:
1. **Do not post anything else for 6 hours.** Let it run.
2. Reply to every single comment within the hour — comments drive reach.
3. Pin a comment with your WhatsApp number.
4. Post a follow-up the next day that starts: *"Since yesterday's video blew up…"*
5. DM everyone who commented offering the free guide.
6. Reinvest KES 500 to boost it on the second day.
