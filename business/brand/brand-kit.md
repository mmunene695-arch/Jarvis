## 2. Why this name

**KaziPesa** = *work* + *money* in Sheng. It says what it does, it is impossible to misspell from hearing it, and it reads as local rather than as a dropshipping storefront.

Rules for a Kenyan service brand:
- Sayable out loud, once, and remembered.
- No numbers or underscores.
- No "Enterprises", "Ventures", "Solutions" or "Global" — they signal a company that exists mainly on paper.

Alternative names if you want one: **Kazi Pap**, **Pesa Pap**, **Nyeri CV Plug**, **Biashara Pap**, **Kazi Haraka**.

## 3. Visual identity

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary | `#0A7D3C` (deep green — M-Pesa-adjacent, reads as money and growth) |
| Dark | `#0D1117` (near-black for headers) |
| Accent | `#25D366` (WhatsApp green — reuse it, it is trusted) |
| Light | `#E8F6EE` (pale green backgrounds) |
| Type | Heavy sans-serif. In Canva: **Montserrat** or **Poppins** for headings, **Inter** for body. |

**Logo:** your name in bold dark, with the last word or a full stop in green.
> **KaziPesa**<span>.</span>

Make it in Canva in 10 minutes. Do not spend longer. A logo has never made anyone a sale.

## 5. Tone of voice

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short sentences | Corporate filler ("we are pleased to offer…") |
| Real numbers | Vague promises ("transform your career") |
| Sheng where natural | Forced slang |
| "You pay after you see the draft" | "Kindly remit payment to facilitate processing" |
| Admitting what you can't do | Overpromising |

You are a person in Nyeri who is good at this and quick. Write exactly like that.
